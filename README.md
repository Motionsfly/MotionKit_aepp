# MotionKit by Motionsfly

All-in-one panel for **After Effects** and **Premiere Pro** (Windows + macOS).

## How to update (existing buyers)
1. Open your **Gumroad library**: https://app.gumroad.com/library
2. Download the new **MotionKit** zip and unzip it
3. Close After Effects / Premiere Pro and run the installer again (Windows: `Install on Windows.bat`, Mac: right click `Install on Mac.command` > Open)
4. Your settings, presets, license and your own light leaks stay
